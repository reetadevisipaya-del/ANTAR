00 / RESEARCH POSITION

The polished interface is the final layer.
The real work happens before it.

I care about the barriers that are easy to dismiss as “user error”: sensory overload, unclear navigation, missing information, inaccessible workflows and systems that assume everyone interacts the same way.

I use psychology, field research and UX methods to make those barriers visible — then turn them into requirements that can actually be designed and tested.
