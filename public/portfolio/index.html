ROLE-AWARE / PARENT · STAFF · ADMIN
A — RESEARCH

Start with the information gaps.

The research problem was not “build another school app.” It was fragmented communication: attendance in one place, reports somewhere else, therapy coordination in messages, documents scattered, and medical support disconnected from the institute experience.

B — SYNTHESIS

Turn needs into a system.

Requirements were grouped around daily visibility, child information, school updates, care coordination, document access, two-way communication and nearby healthcare. The architecture was deliberately role-based so each person sees what they need — not the institution's entire complexity.

C — DEVELOPMENT

Make the research functional.

ANTAR evolved into a React + Supabase product with Parent, Staff and Admin roles, authentication, access control, child/staff assignment flows, daily reports, attendance, messaging, care records and medical-help pathways.

D — TESTING

Evaluate the moments that matter.

AI UXPRODUCT DESIGNFOCUS

NOVA

An AI focus planner for people whose productivity tools became another source of pressure.

Figma ↗ GitHub ↗
AI / FOCUS
CALENDAR → TASKS → JOURNAL
A — PROBLEM

Productivity can become cognitive noise.

NOVA explores a simple tension: the more overwhelmed someone is, the less useful a dense planning system becomes. The product direction reframed “do more” into “decide what deserves attention now.”

B — EXPERIENCE

One loop, not seven disconnected tools.

The information architecture links onboarding, Home, Calendar, Tasks, Journal, AI Focus and Settings into one continuity loop. Planning becomes a progression instead of a collection of tabs.

C — DESIGN SYSTEM

Calm hierarchy with a future-facing edge.

High-fidelity flows, reusable components, micro-interactions and an editorial visual system make the interface feel intelligent without making it feel clinical.

D — USABILITY LENS

Test whether AI actually reduces friction.
